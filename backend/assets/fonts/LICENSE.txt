Tinos — Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic

Copyright © The Tinos Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/tinos)
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

Tinos is a serif typeface metric-compatible with Times New Roman (the
"Times" substitute of the Croscore font family). It is used for the body
text of official generated PDF documents (EP-009-S09 template), giving them
the typographic appearance of a formal administrative document.

Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

Fraunces — Bold

Copyright © 2020 The Fraunces Project Authors (https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces)
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

Fraunces is the display typeface of the web app brand wordmark (« La Ruche »).
Fraunces-Bold.ttf is embedded into the compiled binary at build time and renders
the brand block of the EP-009-S09 PDF template header, matching the app's
typography exactly.

Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

DM Sans — Regular

Copyright © 2014 The DM Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts)
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

DM Sans is the UI typeface of the web app. DMSans-Regular.ttf is embedded into
the compiled binary at build time and renders the header tagline
(« Gestion participative ») and the page footers of the EP-009-S09 PDF template.
